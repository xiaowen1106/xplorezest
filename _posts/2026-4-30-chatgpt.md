As someone who works in tech and coaching, I get this question a lot:
“What’s the difference between talking to a life coach vs. chatting with ChatGPT?”

Here’s the way I’ve come to understand it:

* ChatGPT helps you think better within your current perspective.
A coach helps you see beyond your current perspective.
* ChatGPT is fast, clear, and frictionless.
Coaching is slower, sometimes uncomfortable — but that’s often where real change happens.
* ChatGPT gives you answers.
A coach stays with you until you find the real question.
* And maybe the biggest difference:
ChatGPT is a tool.
Coaching is a relationship.

**But here’s the thing: Coaching is hard to understand until you actually experience it.**

It’s not advice.
It’s not problem-solving.
It’s a space where someone listens, reflects, and challenges you in a way that helps you uncover your own patterns, and move forward differently.
