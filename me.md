<h3>A Life Across Borders</h3>
<p>Born in Beijing, I spent my young adulthood immersed in Europe, particularly the vibrant culture of France. In 2021, I crossed the Atlantic to begin a new chapter in Canada. This multicultural path taught me how to adapt and bridge gaps, but it also meant constantly recalibrating my identity across borders.</p>


<h3>Ten Years in the Code</h3>
<p>For over a decade, my life was defined by logic, data, and the fast-paced world of technology. As a software engineer, I navigated every environment imaginable—from the rigid structures of global banks to the high-pressure sprints of startups and Big Tech. But my story isn't just told in lines of code; it’s told in the many languages and landscapes I’ve called home.</p>


<h3>The Reality of Burnout</h3>
<p>However, beneath the professional success and the global moves, I was struggling. I realized that while I knew how to build complex software, I hadn't yet learned the most vital skill of all: how to truly take care of myself.</p>


<h3>The Wish that Changed Everything</h3>
<p>Last year, at a concert by my favorite singer, the music stopped for a moment. He asked us all to make a wish for ourselves. In that crowd, surrounded by melody, I whispered my truth: "I wish to learn how to take care of myself." That wish became my mission. I transitioned from debugging software to understanding the human heart, beginning my professional life coaching training. I am still in the process of healing—I don’t claim to be "finished," because growth is a lifelong journey. </p>
