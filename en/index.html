01 Coaching conversations

A conversation
to help you see yourself

Life coaching is a supportive conversation built around listening and thoughtful questions. When you are facing a choice, feeling stuck, or ready for change, we can make sense of what is happening, notice familiar patterns, and explore a direction that feels right for you.

You do not need to have everything figured out before we begin. We can start with what matters most to you right now and turn new understanding into a next step in everyday life.

04 About

Hello,
I am Xiaowen

I am a Life Coach and have spent more than a decade working in technology, across teams and workplaces with different rhythms. Moving from Beijing to Europe and then to Canada has continually reshaped how I see myself and deepened my understanding of the complex, real emotions that come with change.

I have often been good at solving complex problems, while also learning how to care for myself and find balance between work and life. My own exploration has taught me that we do not always need someone else to decide for us. Sometimes we need a thoughtful, purposeful conversation to hear ourselves more clearly, see our choices, and reconnect with confidence and resilience.

I hope to offer a sincere conversation and steady support as you step out of familiar patterns, find a direction that feels right for you, and put your energy toward what you truly want to do.
