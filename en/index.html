01 Coaching conversations

A conversation
to help you see yourself

Life coaching is a supportive conversation built around listening and thoughtful questions. When you are facing a choice, feeling stuck, or ready for change, we can make sense of what is happening, notice familiar patterns, and explore a direction that feels right for you.

04 About

Hello,
I am Xiaowen

After years in technology, I became used to breaking down complex problems through rational analysis, and often helped people around me find clarity. Over time, I realized that solving problems is one ability; being present with what you feel is another. Even when everything looked fine from the outside, I have felt unhappy. This taught me that understanding and caring for yourself is a lifelong practice. Some answers cannot be found through analysis alone; we also need to reconnect with ourselves.

This is why I was drawn to coaching conversations. Through listening, empathy, and thoughtful questions, I hope to help you see your feelings, what matters to you, and the possibilities ahead. I will not make decisions for you. Instead, I hope to help you recognize your own strength and become more fully yourself and shape a life that is your own. Life will keep bringing challenges, but you can meet them with trust in yourself, knowing someone is alongside you.
